Act as a senior digital art director, product designer and frontend UX specialist designing a bespoke website for AVANCE LAW PARTNERS, a Bangladesh-based law firm established in 1998. Its audience includes companies, business owners, institutions and individuals. The primary visitor journey is to understand the firm, find relevant legal services, review its lawyers and send an enquiry.

Create a complete, high-fidelity homepage with desktop and mobile layouts, supported by a reusable design system. Use the attached logo and exact brand palette. Make deliberate visual decisions specific to this firm. Prioritize readability, trust, welcoming imagery and a distinctive composition over decorative complexity.

CREATIVE CONCEPT: “Clarity, with presence.”

Draw on the quiet atmosphere of a contemporary legal chamber: dark timber, warm stone, natural daylight, disciplined typography and generous room to read. The signature visual is a “Window of Clarity”: a large architectural image whose subtle pointer reveal echoes the sweeping curve within the supplied ALP monogram. Spend most of the visual drama here. Keep other components calm and precise.

Use an asymmetric composition with an offset image window and clear reading order. Avoid making every section a repeated text-left/image-right split. Give the firm introduction, services, people and contact distinct layouts connected by consistent typography and spacing. Avoid a dense newspaper layout or decorative numbered sections. Services are categories, not ranked steps.

BRAND TOKENS

- Obsidian: #141414 — header, hero, footer, dark text on light backgrounds.
- Charcoal: #2B2B2B — secondary dark surfaces and body text on ivory.
- Gold: #C9A227 — primary action fills, small accents and selected states.
- Ivory: #F6F1E4 — reading surfaces and text on dark backgrounds.

Use dark areas for the opening and closing, with substantial ivory space through the body. Gold should occupy approximately 5–8% of the composition. Keep the metallic rendering inside the supplied logo; use flat gold for interface controls. Avoid extending shiny gold gradients throughout the UI. Use dark text on gold buttons. Do not use gold for small text on ivory or ivory text on gold. Derive muted colors from the palette and verify contrast in every actual state. Decorative dividers can be subtle; input boundaries and focus indicators must remain clearly visible.

LOGO HANDLING

Preserve the supplied monogram, scales, lettering and proportions. Do not redraw it with AI, stretch it, place it inside a generic badge or animate its individual letters. Remove only excess outer black space when preparing a display crop. Use the full lockup at a size where the lettering is readable, particularly in the footer. For a compact header, propose a horizontal variant using the intact ALP monogram beside a readable firm name; label that variant for brand review. Until approved, retain the supplied full lockup and allow sufficient header height. The black image background must sit naturally against its surrounding surface; use a transparent/vector brand asset when the client supplies one.

TYPOGRAPHY AND GEOMETRY

Use Newsreader for major headings and Manrope for body text, navigation, controls and admin UI, subject to availability. Use two font families only. Keep the logo's lettering as artwork. Avoid exaggerated thin strokes, excessive italics and widely tracked body text.

Suggested desktop sizes: hero 68–84 px with approximately 1.06 line height; section headings 40–52 px; body 17–18 px with 1.55–1.7 line height; navigation and controls 15–16 px. Mobile: hero 38–46 px; section headings 28–34 px; body 16–17 px. Adjust for real text wrapping, including long service titles.

Use a 12-column desktop grid, 8-column tablet grid and 4-column mobile grid. Target a 1280 px content maximum, with fluid outer gutters: roughly 64 px on a 1440 px desktop, 32 px on tablet and 20 px on mobile. Use an 8 px spacing rhythm with 4 px adjustments. Section spacing: roughly 96–128 px desktop and 56–72 px mobile. Controls should feel precise, with 4–8 px corner radii. Avoid pill-shaped buttons and floating rounded containers everywhere.

HEADER

Design a premium, persistent header with the brand at left, Home / About / Lawyers / Services / Contact navigation, and a gold “Make an enquiry” action. The header starts against the dark hero and remains readable while scrolling. A slim divider and slight background change can indicate its scrolled state; avoid frosted glass and heavy blur. Do not make a contact action imply an appointment has been booked.

On mobile, show the readable brand and an accessible menu button. The expanded menu contains large links, a visible close control and the enquiry action. Include active page, hover, keyboard focus and expanded states. Keep all actions reachable on short screens.
